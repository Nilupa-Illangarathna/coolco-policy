About This Policy

CoolCo is an air conditioner business and customer service application. It has two parts: a business management tool for CoolCo staff, and a customer-facing section that lets CoolCo's customers manage their AC warranty, book servicing, and track invoices. This Privacy Policy explains how the application handles information for both.

Information We Collect

The application may collect:

  • User account credentials used for authentication (staff), or your mobile number, verified by a one-time SMS code, to identify your customer account
  • Your name and business data entered by users (customers, products, contracts, invoices)
  • Your location, when you choose to pin a service address while booking an AC service visit — not collected in the background
  • Images captured by the device camera or chosen from your photo library when required by application features (e.g. barcode scanning, attaching a photo to a service record)
  • Device storage access for exporting or saving files (PDF, CSV, images)

How We Use Information

Information is used solely for:

  • User authentication and session management
  • Core application functionality
  • Business management operations
  • Improving application performance and reliability

Data Sharing

CoolCo does not sell, rent, or trade user data to third parties. Information may be processed by backend servers necessary for application functionality. No data is shared with advertising networks or analytics services.

Data Security

CoolCo uses AES-256 encryption for locally stored credentials, biometric authentication, and HTTPS for all network communication. Reasonable technical measures are implemented to protect user information.

Data Retention

Data is retained for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the service. Users may contact us to request deletion of their data.

Children's Privacy

CoolCo is intended for business use by adults aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.
